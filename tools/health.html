Health Tools — Evidence-Based Calculators for Wellness and Clinical Use

Understanding your own health metrics — and what they mean — shouldn't require a medical degree or expensive software. Yet most health information online either oversimplifies (giving a number with no context) or overwhelms with jargon. HashmiTools' Health section provides calculators that give you accurate numbers and explain exactly what those numbers mean, who they're most relevant for, and when the result warrants a conversation with a doctor.

These tools are evidence-based, using published formulas from WHO guidelines, clinical research, and medical nutrition textbooks. They are designed for two audiences: (1) health-conscious individuals tracking their own wellness metrics, and (2) medical students and healthcare professionals who need quick reference calculations at the bedside or in study.

Why Health Calculators Matter for Pakistani Users

Pakistan faces a significant and growing burden of non-communicable diseases (NCDs): diabetes (estimated 26% prevalence among adults), hypertension, cardiovascular disease, and obesity — all conditions where early awareness of metrics like BMI, caloric intake, and ideal weight can trigger preventive action.

Yet many Pakistanis lack access to regular clinical check-ups, and when they do see a doctor, the appointment is brief. Having accurate baseline metrics — BMI, TDEE, healthy weight range — before or after a clinical visit helps people have more productive conversations with their healthcare providers and make informed day-to-day decisions about diet and activity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these health calculators accurate?

The mathematical formulas used are those published in peer-reviewed medical literature and endorsed by organisations like WHO and the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics. The results are accurate estimates. Individual variation (muscle mass, bone density, medical conditions, medications) means formula outputs are useful guides, not exact prescriptions.

Can medical professionals use these tools for patient care?

Healthcare professionals can use these as quick reference tools for estimates. For clinical decisions (drug dosing, IV fluid calculations, nutritional therapy prescriptions), always verify calculations using your institution's clinical decision support tools and cross-check with senior clinical guidance. The Medical Toolkit is specifically designed with medical students and clinicians in mind.

Do these tools work for children?

Most adult formulas (BMI categories, Mifflin-St Jeor TDEE, Devine ideal weight) are not valid for children under 18. Paediatric health assessment uses age- and sex-specific growth charts and reference data. The tools on this page are for adults unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Is my health data stored or shared?

No health data you enter (weight, height, age, medical inputs) is stored on our servers or shared with any third party. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. Your health information is private.

What makes the Medical Toolkit different from general health tools?

The Medical Toolkit is designed specifically for medical students and healthcare professionals. It includes clinical scoring systems (GCS, APGAR), drug dosing with maximum dose capping, IV drip rate calculations, BSA-based calculations, 60+ lab reference values, and a pharmacology flashcard system with spaced repetition — features that go beyond general wellness calculators into genuine clinical utility.

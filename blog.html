What You'll Find on the HashmiTools Blog

This blog exists to answer the questions our calculators can't answer on their own. A Zakat calculator tells you how much to pay — our Zakat guide explains which assets are zakatable in the first place, how to treat committee (BC) savings, provident funds, and gold jewellery that is worn regularly. A tax calculator applies the slabs — our FBR tax guide explains the difference between salaried and non-salaried rates, what filer status actually changes, and which deductions people commonly miss.

Every article is written around real decisions our readers face: whether to finance a car or pay cash in Pakistan's interest-rate environment, which bank savings accounts actually beat inflation, how to start freelancing from Pakistan and get paid legally, and how Islamic inheritance shares are actually distributed among heirs under Faraid rules.

Our Editorial Approach

Guides that involve regulations — FBR tax slabs, Nisab values, bank profit rates — are dated in the article and revised when the underlying rules change (typically after the federal budget in June and when the State Bank moves the policy rate). Religious topics such as Zakat and inheritance follow mainstream positions of the four Sunni madhabs, and where madhabs differ we say so explicitly rather than presenting one view as universal. Nothing on this blog is professional financial, legal, or religious advice — for binding decisions, consult a qualified advisor, tax practitioner, or scholar, as covered in our disclaimer.

Have a topic you'd like covered — a confusing tax notice, a banking product you want analyzed, a fiqh calculation question? Send it through our contact page; reader questions drive most of what we publish next.

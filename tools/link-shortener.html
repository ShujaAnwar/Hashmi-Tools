💡 How It Works

This tool uses a hash-based redirect system stored in your browser's localStorage. Short links look like hashmitools.com/s/abc123 and redirect to the original URL when opened. Perfect for sharing on social media, WhatsApp, or any character-limited platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this link shortener really free?
Yes, 100% free with no hidden costs. No account required, no daily limits. All processing happens in your browser.
Do the short links expire?
Links are stored in your browser's localStorage and remain valid as long as you don't clear your browser data. They work best for personal use and sharing with yourself across devices. For permanent public links, we recommend a hosted service like Bitly.
Is my data private?
Completely. No data is sent to any server. Your URLs are stored only in your browser's localStorage on your own device.
Can I use this for WhatsApp links?
Yes! Paste your long WhatsApp link (like https://wa.me/923001234567?text=Hello) and get a short, shareable link perfect for bio pages or messages.

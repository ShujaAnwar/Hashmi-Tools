Frequently Asked Questions

How is EMI calculated?
EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1] where P = Principal loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = loan tenure in months. Our calculator shows the full amortization schedule with every payment breakdown.
What is the SIP Step-Up feature?
Step-Up SIP (also called Top-Up SIP) allows you to increase your monthly SIP amount by a fixed percentage every year. For example, if you start at Rs.5,000/month and increase by 10% annually, by year 5 your monthly investment is Rs.7,321. This aligns your investment with salary growth and dramatically increases wealth accumulation.
What does PITI mean in Mortgage Calculator?
PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance — the four components of a complete mortgage payment. Principal reduces the loan balance, Interest is the lender's charge, Taxes are property taxes held in escrow, and Insurance covers homeowner's and PMI insurance. Our calculator shows each component separately.
Are the exchange rates in Currency Converter live?
Yes, our currency converter fetches live exchange rates from open APIs. The rates are updated every time you load the page. For the most critical financial decisions, we recommend cross-checking with your bank's official rates.

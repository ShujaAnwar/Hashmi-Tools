What Can We Help You With?

Report a calculation issue. If a result from any calculator looks wrong — a tax figure that doesn't match your FBR portal, a Zakat total that differs from your scholar's calculation, an EMI that doesn't match your bank's quote — tell us the exact inputs you used and what you expected. Most discrepancies come from rate updates (budget changes, Nisab movements) and we prioritize these fixes above everything else.

Request a new tool or feature. Many of our tools exist because someone asked for them. If you need a calculator or converter we don't have, describe the problem you're trying to solve rather than just the tool name — it helps us build the right thing.

Report a bug. Include your device, browser, and what happened (a screenshot helps). Because all our tools run in the browser, most issues are fixable quickly once we can reproduce them.

Business, advertising & partnerships. For sponsorship, advertising, or partnership queries, email us directly with "Business" in the subject line. Note that we do not accept paid placement inside calculation results — tool outputs are never influenced by advertisers.

Before you write: answers to the most common questions — "Is it really free?", "Are my files uploaded?", "Which madhab does the inheritance calculator follow?" — are covered on each tool's own FAQ section and in our About page. Privacy questions are answered in detail in our Privacy Policy.

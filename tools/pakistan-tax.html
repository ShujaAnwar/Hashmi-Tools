FAQ

Who is a salaried person for FBR? +
A salaried person is one who derives at least 75% of their income from salary. Salary includes basic pay, allowances, bonuses, and other compensations from employment.
What is the tax-free threshold for salaried persons? +
For tax year 2024-25, income up to ₨600,000 per year (₨50,000/month) is exempt from income tax for salaried individuals in Pakistan.
What is the difference between active filer and non-filer? +
Active filers (those who file annual income tax returns) get lower withholding tax rates on various transactions. Non-filers face higher WHT rates on bank transactions, property, vehicles, and more.

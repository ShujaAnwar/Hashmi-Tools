Frequently Asked Questions

Is this converter free?
Yes, completely free with no limits on number of images or conversions. No registration required.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No! All conversion happens directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy.
What image formats are supported?
JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF are supported. For best results, use JPG or PNG formats.
How many images can I convert at once?
There's no hard limit, but performance depends on your device. For very large numbers (50+), conversion may take a moment. All images become individual pages in the PDF.
Can I change the page order?
Yes! After uploading, drag and drop the image thumbnails to reorder them. The numbered badges show the final PDF page order.

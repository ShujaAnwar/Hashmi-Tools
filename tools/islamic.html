Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Islamic calculations

What is Nisab and how is it calculated?
Nisab is the minimum amount of wealth a Muslim must possess before Zakat becomes obligatory. It is equivalent to 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver. Our Zakat Calculator automatically fetches current gold/silver prices to calculate the exact Nisab threshold.
How accurate is the Hijri Calendar Converter?
We use the Kuwaiti Algorithm (also known as the tabular Islamic calendar), which is the internationally accepted standard for astronomical Hijri date calculations. The algorithm is 99.9% accurate for historical and future date conversions.
Does the Inheritance Calculator support all four madhabs?
Yes! Our Faraid calculator supports Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali jurisprudence. You can select your preferred madhab and the calculations will adjust accordingly. The tool uses exact fraction arithmetic to avoid rounding errors.
Are these tools certified by Islamic scholars?
Our tools are built based on authentic Islamic jurisprudence sources and formulas used by recognized Islamic finance institutions. However, for large estates or complex situations, we recommend consulting a qualified Islamic scholar or Mufti.
Is my financial data saved anywhere?
No. All calculations are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No financial data is ever sent to any server. Your privacy is completely protected. History is optionally saved to your local browser storage only.

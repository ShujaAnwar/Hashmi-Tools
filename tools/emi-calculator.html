EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1) where P = Principal, r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = Total Months

Interest in each installment = Outstanding Balance × Monthly Rate. Principal = EMI − Interest. Balance reduces each month.

How to Edit a PDF Online

  1. Click "Open PDF Editor" and upload your PDF file (drag & drop or click)
  2. Select a tool from the toolbar (text, draw, shapes, highlight, etc.)
  3. Click or drag on the PDF canvas to add your annotations
  4. Use the properties panel on the right to adjust styles
  5. Manage pages using the thumbnail panel on the left
  6. Click "Download PDF" to save your edited file

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF editor free to use?
Yes, 100% free with no registration required. The PDF editor runs entirely in your browser.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No! All processing happens client-side in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.
What PDF features are supported?
You can add text, freehand drawings, shapes, images, highlights, and watermarks. Page management includes reordering, rotating, and deleting pages.
Can I edit existing PDF text?
Due to browser security limitations, you can add new text annotations on top of existing PDF content, but direct editing of original PDF text requires server-side processing not available in this free tool.
What file size is supported?
The tool works best with PDFs up to 50MB. Larger files may be slow to process depending on your device's memory and processing power.

⚙️ How HashmiTools Works — Technical Transparency

Many HashmiTools tools use client-side JavaScript; others require network services. Here is the technology behind the most popular tools:

  • PDF Editor & Merge: Uses PDF.js (Mozilla) for rendering and pdf-lib for editing. Canvas annotations powered by Fabric.js.
  • Image Tools: Uses the HTML5 Canvas API for compression, cropping, resizing, and filter application. No third-party image processing server.
  • QR Code Generator: Uses qrcode.js — all QR generation is local.
  • Finance Calculators: Pure JavaScript arithmetic — no server calls, instant results.
  • PDF Compression: Optimizes PDF image streams and removes redundant data using pdf-lib, entirely in browser.

Some local functions can continue after their libraries have loaded. Network features require a connection and may be unavailable if a provider is down or blocks the request.

Editorial approach and limitations

Our tool guides explain inputs, methods, examples and limitations. A formula-based estimate is not a verified personal recommendation. We do not claim independent professional review unless a named reviewer and the scope of review are shown on the specific page.

  • Source checks: Compare tax and financial information with FBR and State Bank of Pakistan publications for the relevant period.
  • Dated guidance: An article or calculator for a previous tax year is historical guidance, not confirmation of current rates. Check the year and assumptions before using a result.
  • Professional advice: Consult a qualified clinician, tax adviser, lawyer or Islamic scholar for decisions within their field. Read our Disclaimer.
  • AI assistance: AI output may be incomplete or wrong. Check claims, calculations and references independently before relying on it.

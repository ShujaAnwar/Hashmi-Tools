How to Edit a PDF Online for Free

1
Upload your PDF — Drag and drop your file onto the editor, or click "Open PDF" / "Choose PDF File" to browse your device.
2
Choose a tool — Select any tool from the toolbar: Text, Draw, Highlight, Shapes, Signature, Watermark, and more.
3
Edit the PDF — Click or drag on the PDF canvas to add text, highlight content, draw shapes, insert images, or annotate freely.
4
Manage pages — Use the left panel to reorder, rotate, delete, or add pages via drag-and-drop or right-click menu.
5
Download your file — Click "Download PDF" to save the fully edited PDF, or "Download as Images" to export each page as PNG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF editor really free?
Yes, completely free. No signup required, no payment, no hidden fees, and no premium features locked behind a paywall. Every tool in HashmiTools PDF Editor is 100% free to use without any limitations.
Are my PDF files safe and private?
Absolutely safe. Your files are processed entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. No data is ever uploaded to any server — everything happens on your own device. This means your confidential documents stay private.
Can I edit scanned PDFs?
Yes! While scanned PDFs don't have selectable text, you can overlay new text boxes, highlight areas, draw on top, insert images, and add signatures just like any other PDF. The editor works as an annotation layer on top of any PDF content.
What types of edits can I make?
You can add and edit text, freehand draw, highlight content, insert images, add digital signatures, create shapes and arrows, place sticky notes, add watermarks, redact (black out) content, rotate pages, merge multiple PDFs together, and split PDFs into separate files.
How do I save my edited PDF?
Click the blue "Download PDF" button in the top navigation bar. Your edited PDF will be generated in your browser and downloaded instantly to your device with the original filename plus "-edited.pdf". You can also download individual pages as PNG images using the "Images" button.

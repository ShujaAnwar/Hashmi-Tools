About HashmiTools.com

We're on a mission to make professional-grade tools accessible to everyone — completely free, forever.

🎯 Our Mission

Empowering People with Free, Reliable Tools

HashmiTools.com was born from a simple idea: what if there was one place where anyone — from a student in Karachi to a freelancer in New York — could access professional-grade tools for free?

We specifically focus on tools that matter for Muslims and Pakistani users globally — Islamic calculations, Pakistan-specific finance tools, and utility tools with Urdu language support.

Our tools are built with care, accuracy, and a deep respect for the people using them.

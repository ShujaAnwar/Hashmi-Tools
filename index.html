Welcome to HashmiTools — 100+ Free Online Tools, No Signup Required

HashmiTools.com is a free, browser-based toolkit built for students, professionals, small business owners, and everyday users across Pakistan, the UAE, the UK, and beyond. Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your browser — meaning your files never leave your device, no account is needed, and nothing costs money. Whether you need to compress a PDF before emailing it, calculate your monthly loan EMI, generate a QR code for your shop, or find out your daily water intake, you'll find the right tool here in seconds.

The site was built by a developer in Karachi who was frustrated by tools that demanded signups, slapped watermarks on output files, or quietly uploaded sensitive documents to remote servers. HashmiTools was designed differently from the ground up: all PDF editing, image compression, and file conversion uses browser-native JavaScript APIs (PDF.js, pdf-lib, HTML5 Canvas) — no upload endpoint exists on our servers because none is needed. For Pakistani users specifically, you'll find locally-relevant calculators covering FBR income tax slabs, Zakat nisab thresholds, Islamic inheritance (faraidh) calculations, and accurate prayer times using your GPS coordinates.

New tools are added regularly based on user suggestions — the AI hashtag generator, CV builder, water intake calculator, and medical toolkit were all added because real users asked for them. If there is a tool you wish existed here, use the Contact page to suggest it. The most popular tools are featured below, and the full library is organised by category further down the page.

Privacy Policy

Different tools handle data differently. This policy explains what stays on your device and when information is sent to a service.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Who operates this website

This policy covers HashmiTools at www.hashmitools.com. For privacy questions or requests, contact the site operator at hello@hashmitools.com or through our Contact page.

No HashmiTools account is required for public tools. That does not mean visits are anonymous to the hosting provider or to external services you choose to use.

2. Browser processing and external services

  • Local tools: PDF merge, split and editing, image compression, JSON formatting, password generation, and many calculators process the supplied content using JavaScript on your device. Selecting a file in these tools is not itself an upload to HashmiTools.
  • AI writing: The AI writing suite sends your prompt and selected writing settings to Pollinations when you request a result. Do not enter passwords, identity documents, confidential work, or private medical information. The provider processes requests under its own policies.
  • Network utilities: Currency and prayer-time tools request external data. Location-based features can send coordinates to a prayer-time or location provider after you choose the feature. Browser geolocation requires permission; manual location entry is available where the tool supports it.
  • SEO auditing and link shortening: A submitted URL may be shared with the destination site, a public CORS proxy, or a link-shortening provider. Do not submit private URLs, access tokens, or signed document links.
  • Shorts Maker: Video processing requires a separately configured backend. If you connect one and upload, your video is sent to that backend, not processed only in your browser. Connect only a server you trust and check its retention policy before sending a video. No public backend is configured in this version.
  • Downloaded libraries and models: Local tools may first download scripts, fonts or AI model files. These requests expose ordinary connection information to the relevant CDN or model host, even when your selected file stays local.

Review the instructions on the particular tool before using sensitive data. Closing a tab generally releases working memory but does not erase downloaded output files or saved browser data.

3. Contact messages and hosting information

The contact form prepares an email draft locally. It does not submit a message to a HashmiTools form server or confirm delivery. When you send an email or WhatsApp message, we and the communication provider receive the details you include, such as your name, email address, phone number and message.

Contact information is used to reply to enquiries, investigate reported problems and handle privacy requests. Do not include information that is not needed for your enquiry. Contact records can remain in the receiving mailbox; ask us about deletion if needed.

The website is hosted on Vercel. Hosting and network providers can process IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, browser information and diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the service. We do not claim that these technical records are anonymous. Provider retention practices are governed by their policies; see Vercel's privacy policy.

4. Cookies and local storage

Tools may save theme preferences, calculator history, drafts, typing scores, recent tools or connection settings in your browser's localStorage. This information can persist between visits. Do not save confidential information on a shared device. Browser settings let you inspect and delete site data for hashmitools.com and www.hashmitools.com; doing so can remove saved work and preferences.

Automatic Google ad loading is paused in this version, and no Google Analytics tag is installed by the public site code. The previous Accept/Decline banner is not used as advertising consent. Hosting, external links and services you use have separate data practices.

5. Advertising and consent

HashmiTools intends to use Google AdSense. This version retains the publisher account verification meta tag and ads.txt, but does not load Google's advertising script or request ads. These verification files are not consent controls and do not themselves serve advertisements.

When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, IP addresses and other identifiers to serve and measure ads based on visits to this website and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to personalize advertising where allowed and consented to.

Before enabling advertising, the site operator must configure the required consent and privacy messages, including a Google-certified consent management platform for relevant European traffic. Continuing to browse is not treated as consent. Advertising choices must be made available through that platform when ads are enabled.

Learn how Google uses data at Google's partner-site privacy information. You can manage personalized advertising through My Ad Center and participating vendors through Digital Advertising Alliance choices. Opting out of personalization is not the same as blocking all ads or deleting existing cookies.

6. Service providers and international processing

Website delivery uses Vercel, Google Fonts and CDNs such as jsDelivr and cdnjs. Particular tools may also use Pollinations, currency-rate providers, prayer-time APIs, link shorteners or public proxies. They receive the connection details or inputs needed for the requested service and may process them in other countries.

HashmiTools does not sell contact messages. However, we cannot promise that no information reaches third parties: network tools and communication services necessarily receive the information you send them. Follow the tool's service links and review provider terms before use.

7. Your choices, security and children's privacy

You can avoid network-dependent tools, decline browser location permission, delete saved site data and use local-only tools for appropriate tasks. No service or device can guarantee absolute security. Keep your browser updated and do not use public tools for secrets.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information, object to processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Send requests to hello@hashmitools.com, identifying the relevant message or service without sending unnecessary identity documents. We can only act on records we control; provider-held data may require a request to that provider.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information should contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion where applicable.

8. Policy changes

We will update this page when data practices change, including before enabling new advertising or analytics services. The date above identifies the latest revision. Please check it before using a new tool or sharing personal information.

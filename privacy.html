Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2025 | Effective: May 22, 2025

Summary: HashmiTools.com respects your privacy. We don't sell your personal data. Most tools work entirely in your browser. We use Google Analytics and Google AdSense which may use cookies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Usage Data: We automatically collect information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, and features used.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and device type for analytics purposes.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense).
  • Contact Information: If you contact us via our contact form, we collect your name, email, and message content.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • ✅ Financial data entered into calculators (processed in your browser only)
  • ✅ Personal identification numbers or CNIC numbers
  • ✅ Passwords generated using our tools
  • ✅ Documents uploaded to PDF tools (processed client-side)

3. Google AdSense & Analytics

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous data about website usage. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality (e.g., dark/light mode preference).
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics cookies to understand user behavior.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense cookies for targeted advertising.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We encourage you not to share sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability

Contact us at privacy@hashmitools.com to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

HashmiTools.com does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@hashmitools.com

How ATS Scanning Works

When you apply to a mid-to-large company, your resume is first processed by an ATS before reaching human eyes. Here's what happens:

  1. Parsing — the ATS extracts text from your PDF or Word file and categorises it into fields: Name, Email, Work Experience, Education, Skills, etc.
  2. Keyword matching — the system compares your resume text against the job description, looking for keyword overlap. Resumes with low keyword match scores are filtered out automatically
  3. Formatting compliance — tables, text boxes, headers/footers, and creative layouts often fail to parse correctly, causing information to be lost or miscategorised
  4. Ranking — surviving resumes are ranked by match score, and only the top candidates (often the top 10–20%) are shown to a recruiter

Studies show that 75% of resumes are never seen by a human because they fail ATS filtering. Our ATS Score tells you exactly where your resume falls short and what specific changes will increase your score.

Writing High-Impact Resume Bullet Points

The single biggest improvement most resumes need is replacing duty descriptions with achievement statements. Use the Action Verb + Task + Quantified Result formula:

  • Weak: "Responsible for managing social media accounts"
  • Strong: "Grew Instagram following from 2,400 to 18,000 in 8 months through a consistent content calendar and influencer partnerships, increasing monthly website referrals by 340%"

Notice the strong version has: an action verb (Grew), a specific metric (2,400 → 18,000), a timeframe (8 months), a method (content calendar + influencer partnerships), and a business outcome (340% referral increase). Every bullet point should aim to hit at least 3 of these 5 elements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my resume data saved on your servers?

No. All resume data is saved exclusively in your browser's localStorage — on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Your draft persists between sessions on the same device, but clearing browser data will remove it. Use the Export button to save a copy before clearing browser storage.

Do I need a Claude API key for the AI features?

The 4-step wizard, templates, and PDF/Word export work without any API key. The AI Suggestions and ATS Detailed Analysis features require a Claude API key, which you enter once and is stored locally in your browser — never sent to our servers. If you don't have a key, the heuristic ATS score (which scores keyword density, formatting, and section completeness) works without one.

Should I use a one-page or two-page resume?

One page for 0–7 years of experience; two pages for 8+ years of experience. Academic CVs for faculty/research positions are exempt from this rule and can be as long as necessary. Never force a two-page resume onto one page by reducing font size below 10pt or margins below 0.5 inches — this creates an unreadable, cluttered document that is worse than a clean two-pager.

Can I export to Word format for further editing?

Yes. The Word export creates an HTML document with a .doc extension — this opens directly in Microsoft Word and LibreOffice, allowing you to make final manual adjustments. The structure is preserved for ATS compatibility. Note: some complex formatting elements render slightly differently in Word vs. the browser preview.

How should I optimise my resume for a specific job posting?

Read the job description carefully and identify the 5–8 most important keywords (skills, tools, qualifications the employer mentions repeatedly). Mirror this language in your resume — if they say "stakeholder management" use that exact phrase rather than "client relations." Our ATS score compares your content against keywords you specify to help you optimise for specific roles.

💡 Tips to Improve Your Typing Speed

Professional tips to reach 80+ WPM

👆
Touch Typing
Learn to type without looking at the keyboard. Use home row (ASDF JKL;) as your base position for all fingers.
🎯
Accuracy First
Focus on accuracy before speed. Correcting mistakes slows you down more than typing slowly but accurately.
📅
Daily Practice
Practice 15-20 minutes daily for consistent improvement. You can expect 5-10 WPM gain per week with regular practice.
🖥️
Proper Posture
Sit straight, elbows at 90°, wrists relaxed and slightly elevated. Good ergonomics prevents fatigue and improves speed.
🔤
Learn Shortcuts
Master keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+C/V/Z, etc.). These reduce mouse usage and significantly boost overall productivity.
⚡
Look Ahead
Train your eyes to read 2-3 words ahead of where you're typing. This reduces pauses and increases flow.

Frequently Asked Questions

About typing speed and this test

How is WPM calculated?
WPM (Words Per Minute) = (Total correct characters typed / 5) / time in minutes. We divide by 5 because the average English word is 5 characters. So if you type 250 correct characters in 1 minute, your WPM = 250/5 = 50 WPM. This is the industry-standard calculation method.
How is accuracy calculated?
Accuracy = (Correct keystrokes / Total keystrokes) × 100%. Every correct character adds 1 to correct count. Every wrong character adds 1 to total but not correct count. Backspace corrections don't improve accuracy — only correct first-attempt keystrokes count.
What is a good WPM score?
• Beginner: Under 30 WPM
• Average: 40-50 WPM
• Good: 60-80 WPM
• Professional: 80-100 WPM
• Expert: 100-120 WPM
• Elite: 120+ WPM
The world record is 212 WPM by Barbara Blackburn. Most office jobs require 40-60 WPM minimum.
What is the difference between Easy/Medium/Hard levels?
Easy uses common, short everyday words (3-5 letters). Medium uses standard sentences with moderate vocabulary and punctuation. Hard uses complex paragraphs with technical words, numbers, punctuation, and longer sentences. Start with Easy and progress to harder levels as your accuracy improves.
Can I share my typing results?
Yes! After completing a test, you can share your results via Twitter, WhatsApp, or copy the result text. Your score includes WPM, accuracy percentage, and the difficulty level so others can see and compare. Your personal best scores are saved locally in your browser.

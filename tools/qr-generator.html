FAQ

Are these QR codes permanent? +
Yes! Static QR codes (URL, text, WiFi) are permanent and will work forever. They don't expire. However, if you link to a URL and that URL changes, the QR code will point to the old URL.
What size should I use? +
For print, use at least 300×300 pixels at 300 DPI. For websites, 200×200 is sufficient. For billboards or banners, use 500×500 or higher.
What is error correction level? +
Error correction allows QR codes to be read even when partially damaged or obscured. Higher levels (H=30%) are more reliable but create more complex codes. Use H level if your QR code might get scratched or dirty.
